## Markan priority

https://www.google.co.uk/books/edition/Theological_and_Theoretical_Issues_in_th/uuXqDwAAQBAJ?hl=en&gbpv=1&pg=PA89&printsec=frontcover

> ... the widespread (though not quite universal) acceptance of Markan priority [note 1], ...

Where note 1 is:

> As part of a recent paper delivered as part of the Q section at the 2017 SBL
annual meeting in Boston, MA on “The State of Q Research in Britain,”
I surveyed current academic colleagues holding New Testament posts in Britain
on their positions in relation to various questions relating to the Synoptic
Problem. The answer to one of the questions “Which Gospel do you think was
written first?” attracted near universal consensus. Only one of the twenty-six
respondents had any degree of hesitation about Markan priority. The other
twenty-five stated that they thought Mark to be the first  gospel to be
written, without any qualification.
