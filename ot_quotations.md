# Old Testament Quotations in the Four Gospels

Accepted (clear) Old Testament quotations in Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John.

## Inventory basis

The entry list matches the **UBS Greek New Testament (4th ed.) Index of Quotations** (NT order), which restricts itself to clear quotations and excludes mere allusions. Cross-checked against:

- UBS4 Index of Quotations (primary inventory): **107** gospel entries (Matthew 48, Mark 21, Luke 24, John 14), counting separate UBS sub-rows such as Matt 19:19 (Lev 19:18) beside Matt 19:18–19.
- Kalvesmaki’s [NT/LXX/MT chart](https://kalvesmaki.com/LXX/NTChart.htm) of “obvious quotes” (narrower; omits many antitheses and legal citations that UBS includes).
- Kiwi Hellenist gospel citation catalogue (broader; includes formulaic / generic citations outside UBS).
- Working text-type notes in `synoptic_quotes.md` (Archer–Chirichigno / Menken / Jones).

**Deliberately omitted** (present in some popular charts but **not** in the UBS Index of Quotations): Matt 27:35 (Ps 22:18 narrative echo); Mark 9:44 (textually doubtful); Mark 15:28† (spurious); Luke 1:17 (Malachi allusion). Other Kiwi “G/QC” items without a discrete quoted text (e.g. Matt 2:23 Nazorean; Matt 24:15; Luke 21:22) remain in the `synoptic_quotes.md` supplement rather than here.

For each quotation:

1. **Heading** — Gospel reference
2. **MLV** — Modern Literal Version (2026) with surrounding context; quoted words in *emphasis*
3. **Old Testament** — location(s); multiple candidates listed where relevant
4. **LXX** — matching text from Brenton’s English Septuagint
5. **Source type** — best guess (LXX, revised/mixed Septuagint, hebraizing, indeterminate, free)

**1:22** Now this whole thing has happened in-order-that what was said by the Lord through the prophet might be fulfilled, saying, **1:23** *Behold, the virgin will be holding a child in her womb , and will be bearing a son, and they will call his name Immanuel*; which is, after being translated, *God with us.* **1:24** Now Joseph was awakened from his sleep and did as the messenger of the Lord commanded him and took his wife to him;

**LXX (Brenton, Isa 7:14):** Therefore the Lord himself shall give you a sign; behold, a virgin shall conceive in the womb, and shall bring forth a son, and thou shalt call his name Emmanuel.

**Source type:** LXX (Septuagint)
